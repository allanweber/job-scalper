Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026 · Version 2026-08-23

Job Scalper helps you find remote tech jobs and prepare applications. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. Job Scalper is operated by Allan Weber. Questions: a.cassianoweber@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account identity. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, and Google account identifier, used to create and secure your account.
  • Your resume. If you upload a resume we store the file and the text extracted from it. A resume can contain sensitive personal information; we treat it as sensitive and use it only to match jobs to you and to generate application materials you request.
  • Profile & preferences. The search profile derived from your resume and the settings you choose (job sources, notification preferences, salary and location preferences).
  • Activity in the app. Jobs you view, save, and draft applications for, and the status you set on each application.
  • Generated materials. The tailored resumes and cover letters you create. These are private to your account.
  • Device & notifications. If you enable notifications, a device messaging token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send match alerts.
  • Usage metadata. Counts of AI actions you take, to enforce plan limits and keep the service running.

2. How we use your information

  • Match remote jobs to your profile and show a scored feed.
  • Generate tailored resumes and cover letters when you ask.
  • Send notifications you opt into.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the service and enforce usage limits.

3. AI processing and third parties

To generate tailored application materials, we send the relevant job posting and your resume text to an AI provider — Anthropic and/or OpenAI — which processes them on our behalf to produce your draft. We use Google for sign-in and, if you enable notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver them. The service is hosted on a private server. We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Optionally, you may provide your own AI provider key. If you do, we store it encrypted and use it only to run your own AI requests.

4. Job postings

Job postings are public, non-personal data aggregated from many sources. They are shared across the service and are not tied to your identity.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data until you delete it (see below). Aggregated job postings are trimmed automatically on a schedule.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Personal AI provider keys are encrypted at rest. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account and data. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → Delete account) or from our web deletion page. This permanently removes your identity, resume, profile, drafts, and associated personal data.
  • Access & correction. You can view and update your profile in the app.
  • Notifications. You can turn notifications on or off at any time.

If you are in a region with data-protection rights (for example the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may exercise those rights by contacting us.

8. Children

Job Scalper is intended for people aged 18 and older and is not directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the version and effective date above; material changes may also be surfaced in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: a.cassianoweber@gmail.com.
