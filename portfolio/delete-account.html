Job Scalper · account & data deletion

You can permanently delete your Job Scalper account and the data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open Job Scalper and go to Profile → Delete account. Your account and data are removed right away, no email needed.

Option 2 — By request (no app needed)

If you've uninstalled the app or can't sign in, email us and we'll delete your account for you.

Email a deletion request

Send it from, or tell us, the Google email address you sign in with, so we can verify the account is yours. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Both options permanently remove the personal data tied to your account:

  • Your account identity (name and email from Google sign-in)
  • Your uploaded resume and the text extracted from it
  • Your search profile and preferences
  • Your saved jobs, generated drafts, and application history
  • Any device notification tokens and personal AI provider key

What may be kept

Public, non-personal job postings are not tied to you and remain in the shared catalog. We may retain limited records where required to prevent abuse or meet legal obligations; these are minimized and not used to re-identify you.
